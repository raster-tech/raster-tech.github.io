Installing

Windows says "Windows protected your PC."

The beta builds aren't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. You only do this once on each computer.

My Mac won't open the app.

Right-click the app and choose Open, then Open again. Or try to open it once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

My antivirus flagged it.

Unsigned beta builds sometimes get flagged. Check that you downloaded it from the release page. If you're unsure, email us before allowing it.

After updating, it asks me to sign in and pick my sheet again.

That's expected from version 0.1.10 onward: the app now opens only the sheets you pick. Live monitoring pauses until you pick it, so update between shows, not during one.

How do I update?

Use Check for Updates in the app, or download the newest installer from the release page. It replaces the old version in place. On Windows, if the old copy won't quit, end it in Task Manager first.

Signing in

Sign-in doesn't work.

Check you picked the right show sheet in the file picker. If it still doesn't work, email us the account you're using.

Which sign-in should I use, Show Device or Engineer Device?

Show Device is the easy way for most people: sign in with your own Google account. Engineer Device uses a service account you create yourself, so you're not signing in with your own account and don't get a picker prompt for every sheet — see the beta guide for setup.

It keeps asking me to sign in again.

That shouldn't happen often now. If it does, click Sign in again — your settings are kept — and let us know, since it may be a bug worth fixing.

Which Google account should I use?

The one that can open your show's tech sheet.

Audits and the dashboard

A device shows "not checked" instead of Failed.

It's on a VLAN your laptop can't reach from the port it's plugged into. The app says "not checked" rather than guessing. Plug into a port on that VLAN, or a trunk, to check it.

A device I know is working shows Failed.

Check that the sheet has its IP address, not a .local name. Many AV switches don't pass .local traffic. Then check the laptop is on the right network. One missed ping never fails a device on its own; it has to miss several in a row.

Lots of devices went red at once.

The dashboard shows a banner when that happens. It usually means the laptop lost its connection (a cable, a switch port, Wi-Fi taking over) rather than half the rig dying. Check the laptop's own connection first.

The screen says STALE.

The data is old because the app hasn't refreshed recently. Nothing you're seeing is being passed off as current. It clears on the next good update. If it doesn't, check the laptop is still on the show network.

After updating, some green devices turned red.

Version 0.1.6 fixed a Windows problem where a device that was switched off could still show green. Red now means it really didn't answer.

How do I leave a VLAN out of the show?

Put a Y in the Hide VLAN row, at the top of the IP INDEX tab, under that VLAN. Those devices show as Skipped.

How do I see the dashboard on my phone?

Connect the phone to the same network as the laptop and open the dashboard address shown in the app.

My phone can't reach the dashboard (Windows laptop).

Windows may have blocked the app at its firewall, which is common right after upgrading from Neon Tech Sheet, because Windows treats the renamed app as new. Open Windows Security → Firewall & network protection → Allow an app through firewall and tick Private for Raster Tech Sheet. Or click Allow the next time Windows asks.

On a Mac

macOS asked about "local network" access.

Say yes. The app needs it to reach your show gear.

I'm not getting alerts.

Allow notifications for the app in System Settings → Notifications.

Your data

Where is my show data kept?

In your own Google Sheet, and nowhere else. We don't keep a copy.

Does it need the internet during a show?

No. It works on the show network. It only needs the internet to sign in and to read and write your sheet.

Does it change anything in my sheet?

It writes only your devices' Status, Last Check and Audit Details (and a MAC address, if that cell was blank), plus a list of unexpected gear on its own tab. Everything else in your sheet is read, never changed.
