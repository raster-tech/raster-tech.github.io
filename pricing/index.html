Questions

Is it per person?

No. One subscription covers your whole company. Add as many people as you need.

What happens if I stop paying?

Nothing breaks during a show. After a 14-day grace period the app goes read-only: the dashboard and phone view keep working, but it won't run new audits or write to your sheet. Your sheet is yours and stays exactly as it was.

Does it need the internet during a show?

No. It works on the show network.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, once payments start: a full refund within 30 days, no questions asked.

Do you do UK pricing?

Yes. Prices are shown in pounds, VAT included where it applies.
