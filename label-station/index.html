Label Station

Label Station prints device, cable and case labels straight from your show's tech sheet — no separate labeling software, and no retyping what's already in your gear index. Built for shop prep — the few weeks before load-in, not the run itself. Most productions already expense a label printer and tape; this fits the same line item.

It's in development. The first label type already works on a real show; the rest (cable and case labels) are being built next.

Want to know when it's ready for other engineers to try? Email support@rastertech.app and we'll let you know — no signup, no list, just a note when it's ready.
